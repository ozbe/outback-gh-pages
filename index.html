Outback

The web, on your wrist.

Outback is a small web browser for Apple Watch. It keeps your bookmarks, folders and history on the watch, so a page is a few taps away, and it syncs them with an iPhone app where you can add, rename, move and reorder what you've saved.

Join the beta on TestFlight
Install TestFlight on your iPhone, then open the link there; the watch app comes with it. Needs iOS 27 and watchOS 27.

Save a page from Safari or the share sheet on your iPhone and it turns up on your watch. Search with the engine you like, and open a bookmark from a complication, a Smart Stack widget or Siri.

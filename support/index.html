Support

Questions, problems, or suggestions about Outback? Email outback@ozbe.dev and I'll get back to you.

It helps to include your Apple Watch model, your watchOS and iOS versions, and whether the problem is on the watch or the phone.

Trying the beta? Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app works too, and screenshots help.

Getting around a page

  • Double tap (pinch your finger and thumb together twice) to pick out the next link, button or field, then tap anywhere to open it.
  • Swipe in from the left edge of the screen to go back, and from the right edge to go forward.
  • Scroll up to bring back the buttons. The menu has Forward, Stop Loading and Reload.

A page looks wrong

Outback draws pages with its own renderer, which doesn't yet handle every site. Some things to try:

  • Turn on JavaScript in Outback's Settings. Some sites need it to show anything.
  • Turn on System Browser in Settings to open pages in watchOS's own browser instead.

Please email me the page's address either way. Pages that look wrong are the most useful thing to hear about.

Bookmarks not appearing

Bookmarks sync between your devices through iCloud, and between the watch and the phone directly. Check that both are signed into the same Apple Account and that iCloud is on for Outback in Settings.

Sites that won't stay signed in

With Private Browsing on, Outback keeps no cookies or website data, so sites can't remember you. Turn it off in Outback's Settings if you'd like to stay signed in.

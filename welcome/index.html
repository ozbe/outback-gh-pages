Welcome to Outback

Welcome to your first Outback bookmark! This page is drawn by Outback's own renderer, which lays out the web for a screen the size of your wrist. Turn the Digital Crown to scroll.

Getting around

  • Tap a link to open it. Or double tap (pinch your finger and thumb together twice) to pick out the next link, button or field, then tap anywhere to open it.
  • Swipe in from the left edge to go back, and from the right edge to go forward.
  • Scroll up to bring back the buttons. The menu in the corner has Reload, Bookmark, Share and Open in System Browser.

Finding things

On the home screen, tap the search field and type an address or a few words. Searches go to DuckDuckGo to start with; you can pick another in Settings. Start with an engine's prefix to use it just this once: "w kangaroo" searches Wikipedia.

Saving pages

Choose Bookmark from a page's menu, or swipe right on a page in History. On your iPhone, tap Share in Safari and choose Outback, and the page is on your watch a moment later. The Outback app on your iPhone is the place to rename, move and reorder bookmarks, and to make folders.

A few more things worth knowing:

  • Add an Outback complication or Smart Stack widget to open a bookmark from your watch face.
  • Ask Siri to "Open a bookmark in Outback", or use Shortcuts or the Action button.
  • If a page looks wrong, try turning on JavaScript in Settings, or System Browser to use watchOS's own browser.
